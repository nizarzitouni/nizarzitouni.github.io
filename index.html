Now

Building farm software at Amaya AG

I'm a Senior Flutter developer at Amaya AG, working remotely on the mobile side of their agritech platform. Cooperatives, input distributors and plantations use it to collect data in the field and get it back to headquarters, across 8 markets.

The apps are offline-first, because fields rarely have signal: agents keep working without a connection and everything syncs once they are back in range. The codebase has grown to around 36 features.
